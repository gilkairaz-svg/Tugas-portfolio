About

A Little About Me

Hi, I'm Khaila Razbani — a digital art enthusiast who enjoys drawing characters and imagining the worlds they live in. This space is where I gather my illustrations and keep experimenting with colour, mood and style.

I am also an active student majoring in Information Systems and Technology Education at the UPI Purwakarta campus.
